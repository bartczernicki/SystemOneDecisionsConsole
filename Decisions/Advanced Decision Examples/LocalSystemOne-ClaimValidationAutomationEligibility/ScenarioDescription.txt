Claim Validation and Automation Eligibility

Four fictional property claims demonstrate how a decision model evaluates completeness, consistency, possible duplication, and eligibility for automated continuation.
It assigns probabilities to continuing the workflow, requesting information, duplicate review, or specialist review.
A deterministic policy requires at least 90% for both confidence and selected-treatment probability, complete and consistent records, and a USD claim amount at or below $5,000 before permitting continuation.
Missing information, duplicate concerns, uncertainty, and mandatory-review conditions lead to information requests or human review.
The console shows probabilities and policy reasons for each simulated action without approving, denying, or settling a claim.
