Predict whether one prospect will subscribe, then use that Bernoulli success probability p to forecast conversions across 100 comparable prospects: X ~ Binomial(100, p).

The application calculates the count distribution and P(X > 30), meaning at least 31 conversions, locally from one model response. It also displays the expected count, 100p. It does not sample outcomes or make 100 model calls.

This synthetic example assumes independent prospects with the same conversion probability and a 30-day subscription window. The forecast is conditional on the estimated p; uncertainty in p and differences or dependencies among real prospects are not modeled.
